# RiyasTech Hub

Develop a fully responsive, modern web application named 'RiyasTech Hub' featuring a futuristic dark-mode UI with vibrant neon blue (#00F0FF) and purple (#8A2BE2) accents, glowing PCB trace background patterns, sleek glassmorphism cards, interactive hover effects, and smooth page transitions.



DESIGN & THEME RULES:

- Primary Theme: Cyber-Dark (#0D1117) with glowing Neon Cyan (#00F0FF) and Deep Violet (#7928CA) gradients.

- Typography: Clean Sans-Serif font (Inter / Poppins) with high contrast for optimal readability.

- Layout: Fully responsive grid layout optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop views.



CORE ARCHITECTURE & PAGE FLOW (10-STEP INTEGRATED FLOW):



1. Page 1 (Brand Landing Screen - Unique First Impression):

 - Hero section featuring an animated electronics/PCB trace background.

 - Prominent Branding: 'RiyasTech Hub' logo with institutional subtitle: "Kumaraguru College of Technology".

 - A sleek 'Get Started / Enter Platform' button that routes to Page 2.



2. Page 2 (Secure Sign-In, Authentic OTP Gatekeeper & Location):

 - Glassmorphic Sign-In card with inputs for Username, Mobile Number, District Dropdown Selection, and an 'OTP Send/Verify' system.

 - STRICT VALIDATION GATEKEEPER: The 'Proceed' or 'Next' button MUST remain disabled until a valid OTP is entered and verified and a district is selected. No dummy bypassing allowed.



3. Page 3 (User Academic Profile):

 - Department Selection: ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, and Core Hardware Branches ONLY (Strictly exclude CSE).

 - Year of Study Selector: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th Year, or 12th Grade / School Student.



4. Page 4 (Advanced Hardware Project Analysis Engine):

 - Form for 'Project Title' and 'About this Project' text area.

 - Multilingual AI Processing: Supports project descriptions in English, Tamil, or Tanglish.

 - Interactive 'Search / Analyze' button with glowing loading animation.

 - Exhaustive Hardware Generation Engine: Immediately generates a complete, project-specific hardware list (ICs, microcontrollers, semiconductors, sensors, motors, dev boards, relays, passive elements, etc.) with zero static/generic shortcuts.



5. Page 5 (Component Selection & Live Multi-Store Ordering Flow):

 - Itemized hardware list with 'Add to Cart' and direct 'Buy Now' buttons.

 - Multi-Store E-Commerce Routing: 'Buy Now' redirects directly to the specific product page on major Indian hardware stores (Robu.in, Amazon.in, Evelta, Probots, ThinkRobotics, and ElectronicsComp).

 - Side-by-Side Dynamic Price & Vendor Comparison Dashboard.



6. Page 6 (Real-Time Refer & Earn + Authentic Admin Hub):

 - User Referral Hub: Generates a unique referral link per registered user. Tracks genuine bonus credits and successful invites ONLY upon authentic new user sign-up via referral links (No pre-filled dummy/fake 1200/1500 points).

 - Admin Hub (Positioned directly below Referral Hub): Real-time overview monitoring actual verified platform registrations, referral links, and distributed credit metrics.



7. Page 7 (Exhaustive Component Guide, AI Troubleshooting & Massive Viva Module):

 - Exhaustive Component Guide: Detailed functional overviews, specifications, and circuit roles for every required component.

 - AI Troubleshooting Assistant: Accepts circuit/hardware doubt queries in Tamil, English, or Tanglish and generates step-by-step diagnostic solutions.

 - Comprehensive Viva & Self-Assessment Module: Generates a massive question bank (100 to 500 project-specific questions and detailed answers) covering hardware concepts, circuit schematics, and project defense diagnostics.



8. Page 8 (Riya AI Chatbot & Dashboard Assistant):

 - Unique Riya AI Chatbot Module: Prominently positioned on the dashboard with a custom hardware/circuit-themed tech icon.

 - Functionality: Configured as a specialized expert in hardware, electronic components, circuit troubleshooting, and hardware implementation (similar to Gemini/ChatGPT) answering all queries in detail.



9. Page 9 (About the Owner):

 - Sleek profile card with an Owner Avatar Frame showing a young South Indian male tech student with short dark hair, wearing a clean white formal shirt against a bright background (`src="owner-profile.jpg"` or direct upload slot).

 - Owner Details:

   * Name: Riyas U

   * Role: ECE 1st Year Student

   * Institution: Kumaraguru College of Technology



10. Page 10 (YouTube Learning Channels, Rating & Contact Us):

 - Technical YouTube Channels: Categorized lists for Top 10 English and Top 10 Tamil technical channels for Robotics and Electronics.

 - Interactive Star-Rating & Feedback widget (Tamil, English, or Tanglish support).

 - Official Contact Section: riyasusman2008@gmail.com with a 'Send Direct Message' action trigger.
